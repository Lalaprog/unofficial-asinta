# BALE / Asinta — client meeting demo

This is a conversation guide, not proof of deployment, acceptance, performance, or evaluation. Use a permitted test account and records the client is comfortable seeing. The optional **?** in the top bar explains the current page; it never changes records. Choose Skip or close it at any time. The new guides and due-date fix must first be released before they appear in production.

## Before the meeting

- Confirm which environment and accounts you are using. A preview can connect to live data; do not assume it is isolated. Avoid showing private data unnecessarily.
- Have the Founder and Supervisor sign-ins available without sharing passwords in the meeting. Confirm their access and connection before starting.
- Plan a read-only demonstration. Do not submit attendance, create payroll, confirm payments, delete/remove workers, create records or send messages just to demonstrate unless separately authorized and safe test data is available. Opening forms and cancelling is safer, but confirm no changes were submitted.
- On both a phone and desktop, try **?**, Next, Back and Skip. Some sections may be empty, unavailable or still loading, in which case the guide says the section is not visible.

## Suggested order (about 15–20 minutes)

1. **Founder dashboard:** Explain balances, workforce/pay, recorded project costs, project filter and items needing attention. Figures are based on available records; the project estimate is entered manually.
2. **Projects → open a site:** Show site details and project tabs. Explain that the project spending view adds project-associated expenses, not an automatic total of all payroll and tool costs. Tools are tracked by project/location, not assigned to a named worker.
3. **Workforce and Roll Call:** Show a project’s worker list, the workforce roster, and Roll Call site/date selection. Explain assigned teams and that a draft is not submitted attendance. The Founder may need to review Supervisor attendance. Show Attendance History without modifying a sheet.
4. **Payroll:** Show the selected-period attendance matrix and existing vouchers. Explain that the Founder initiates a payroll run and reviews vouchers; attendance does not automatically run payroll. Do not generate or mark anything paid during the read-only demo.
5. **Bale requests:** With the Supervisor, show the request form and explain that it sends a structured request to the Founder. With the Founder, show Requests and Issued advances. Approval, actually handing over money, and recording payment/issuance are separate. Do not submit or confirm a payment during the demo.
6. **Expenses:** Show manual entry and the text/receipt-assisted draft flow without saving. Explain that AI proposes fields and categories; the Founder checks and corrects them. A Bale suggestion or flag is not a request approval or payment. Existing receipt photos are accessed from the expense record; the project receipt panel does not currently open the photo directly.
7. **Invoices and follow-ups:** Show an existing invoice, balance, message preview and reminder queue without recording a payment or sending. Automatic SMS/email reminders are on hold; manual sending is available subject to configuration. A due-today invoice is not overdue merely because the clock passed midnight UTC (the fix is pending release).
8. **Tools, accounts and settings:** Show equipment condition and location, role/site assignments, and message templates or security settings as appropriate. Avoid account changes, test emails or message sends. A removed worker retains history, but removal deactivates project assignments and excludes the worker from future payroll runs, including unprocessed attendance; restoration is inactive and does not restore assignments.
9. **Supervisor view:** Show only assigned projects, worker management, Roll Call and Bale requests. Do not imply the Supervisor has Founder finance or client permissions.

If time is short, prioritize project → Roll Call → payroll → Bale → expense → invoice, using the ? guides where useful. Avoid making claims about speed, AI accuracy, security or usability ratings without actual evidence.

## Clarify with the client

1. Is the current **structured Bale request → Founder approval → payment confirmation** workflow acceptable, or should a free-text AI helper be added? What information should it extract and how should mistakes be corrected?
2. Should the Founder see a **suggested** priority for requests? If yes, which concrete, fair rules and exceptions should be used? Approval remains the Founder’s decision; no threshold has been agreed.
3. What should “total project spending” include: recorded site expenses, payroll, equipment purchases/rentals and other costs? How should Bale deductions be treated to avoid double-counting?
4. Is a manually entered rough project estimate sufficient, or is a calculated estimate needed? What information would support a calculation?
5. Should tools remain assigned to sites, or must they be assigned to individual workers? Should costs be entered in Tools or continue as Equipment expenses?
6. Are manual reminders sufficient for now? If automatic reminders are requested, agree on schedules, channels, message approval, provider ownership, safety checks and costs before any activation.
7. Does the client expect invoice line items and totals, or is the current single invoice amount sufficient? What is the required receipt-photo access from a project?
8. Confirm the Supervisor’s intended access, one-site operating preference, handling of past attendance/payroll and how any duplicate worker profiles should be reconciled.

Record decisions and any requested changes. Afterward, align the manuscript with the agreed system scope; check substantial objective changes with the adviser. Do not fill evaluation sections with invented results.
